# Solar Voxel — the voxel skybox candidate

A retrofuturistic solarpunk city in the style of an Akira Toriyama
background, built as ~52k instanced voxels in Three.js and pre-rendered to
pixel art. The first 800 px is the single-screen hero crop; the middle band is
kept pale so the dark gameplay platforms stay readable.

## Regenerate

```bash
node scripts/skybox/voxel/generate.mjs
```

The script bundles `layout.js` + `scene.js` with esbuild, launches headless
Chromium through Playwright, renders each layer at **800x300**, nearest-
neighbour upscales 2x to **1600x600**, quantises every opaque pixel to
`art/skybox/palette.json`, writes the PNGs, hashes them and writes
`manifest.json`. If Chromium is missing:

```bash
pnpm exec playwright install chromium
```

No package.json change is needed; esbuild is resolved from the existing pnpm
store.

## What is in the picture

| Layer | Parallax | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| `sky.png` | 0.015 (drift 0.6) | dithered noon gradient, sun, ringed planet, chunky cumulus, airships, birds |
| `far.png` | 0.06 | pale jade skyline, grand domes and spires |
| `mid.png` | 0.18 | ivory towers, aqueducts, waterfalls, pools, waterwheel, bridge, giant trees, solar arrays, wind turbines |
| `falls.png` | 0.30 + `align: "mid"` | 6 frames × 48x240 of animated water |
| `near.png` | 0.50 | dark framing foliage, rooftops, planters, railings and flowers |
| `contact.png` | — | flattened review composite (frame 0 of both falls) |

The near layer ships with `"alpha": 0.2` in the manifest. That is the
readability hand-off from [`specs/skybox.md`](../../../specs/skybox.md): at full
strength its dark foliage sits in the fighter's value band, and the pale mid
city is the better silhouette carrier. The art is unchanged; the framing is
just pulled back. `haze` is 1.15 for this candidate — the denser city needs a
touch more atmospheric lift than the hand-painted one.

The hero crop runs left to right: seam garden → tall onion-domed tower →
aqueduct and waterfall → pool, waterwheel and curved bridge → water tower →
the giant banyan. The wide panorama adds the great civic dome, a pink blossom
tree, a second aqueduct and a large wind turbine.

## Palette

- Source: `art/skybox/palette.json`, `solarpunk-noon`, **65 colours**.
- Every instance colour is one of those 65; `layout.js` throws if it is not.
- Per-face brightness is baked into the box geometry's vertex colours
  (top 1.0, front 0.97, sides 0.88/0.78, bottom 0.70), then the whole render
  is snapped back to the palette. No opaque pixel is pure black or pure white.
- The sky gradient uses ordered Bayer dithering; voxel layers are quantised
  without dithering so their faces stay flat.
- Transparency is binary (alpha 0 or 255); no soft edges. The canvas PNG
  encoder stores zeroes in fully transparent pixels (normal for RGBA PNG);
  those pixels are not part of the art.

## Tiling

The camera has **no yaw**, so world x maps exactly to screen x. Every cube
that crosses x=0 or x=1600 is duplicated at x±1600 before rendering; clouds,
foliage, buildings and the falls all share that rule. The seam-check file is
`scripts/skybox/voxel/seam-check.png` (four layers tiled across the seam).

The generator also prints a numerical wrap check, comparing the 1599→0
boundary against all 1,600 column boundaries:

| Layer | seam mean | p95 of boundaries | seam percentile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sky | 5.17 | 7.47 | 57.7 |
| far | 1.36 | 8.01 | 74.6 |
| mid | 0.52 | 13.47 | 53.4 |
| near | 0.68 | 3.28 | 71.9 |

A seam that cut a silhouette would rank at the very top of that distribution;
none does.

## Falls

- 6 frames, each **48x240**; frame 0 is the top of the fall.
- Authored at 24x120 and nearest-upscaled 2x, so the water sits on the same 2x
  pixel grid as the panoramas.
- The scroll pattern has a 96 px period and advances 16 px (32 final px) per
  frame; widths, mist pulse and foam ridges all use a 6-frame periodic phase,
  so frame 5 → 0 has the same step as every other pair.
- Manifest items are at `(276, 260)` and `(896, 300)`, aligned to the two
  aqueduct spouts in `mid.png`. The strip carries `factor: 0.3` per the
  contract **and** `align: "mid"` because the runtime (per
  `specs/skybox.md`) pins a waterfall to the layer it falls out of; the
  resolved factor is therefore 0.18, which keeps the water attached when the
  camera scrolls.

## Iteration history

1. **Blockout.** Four layers, about 61k cubes. Clouds were low tan slabs, the
   mid layer was a wall of near-identical towers, the tree read as broccoli,
   and the left fall had no aqueduct mouth.
2. **Sky and face light.** Face shading was raised (front 0.9 → 0.97) so the
   ivory stayed ivory, clouds were rebuilt as jittered stacks of cream lobes
   with a thin peach underside, and the skyline was opened into a few
   landmarks with sky between them.
3. **Hero story.** The pavilions blocking the hero's lower-left were removed;
   the pool was enlarged, the curved bridge moved over it, and a waterwheel,
   teal-glazed domes, a blossom tree, a second wind turbine and a foreground
   railing were added. Vines were reduced and darkened.
4. **Far layer.** Its repeated fins were removed, the domes were simplified to
   solid pale volumes, and the gold floating caps were deleted.
5. **Final pass.** Falls loop made strictly periodic across 5→0, seam metrics
   added, transparent pixels given the indigo palette value, final QA on
   dimensions, palette membership, alpha and hashes.

## Verification

- `sky.png`, `far.png`, `mid.png`, `near.png`, `contact.png`: 1600x600.
- `falls.png`: 288x240; every item fits inside 1600x600 with 48x240.
- `sky.png` and `contact.png` are fully opaque; the other layers use binary
  alpha only.
- Every opaque pixel is exactly one of the 65 palette colours; no opaque pixel
  is pure black or pure white.
- Manifest versions are the first 12 hex chars of each PNG's SHA-256.
- `scripts/skybox/voxel/seam-check.png` is the human-readable double-tile
  check; the table above is the numerical one.

## Weaknesses

- The far layer is a supporting silhouette; its skyline is simpler and more
  repetitive than the mid city.
- The hero's pool/waterwheel/bridge cluster is the busiest area in the
  bottom-left; it reads, but it asks for more attention than the quiet middle
  band strictly wants.
- The middle band still contains the hero tree and the aqueduct. They are
  low-contrast cream/green masses rather than fine detail, but they are not
  empty.
- Tree canopies are chunky voxel masses, not individual leaves.
- The waterfall is 48 px wide by contract; it reads as a narrow ribbon next to
  the aqueduct's arches.
